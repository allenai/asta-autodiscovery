Archaeology Information Graph POC

Hypothesis generation as structured search over conditional-association queries H(A,B | N) on the DiscoveryBench archaeology time series — Bronze Age wealth indicators, population and landscape openness in northern Germany. Three search policies spent an identical budget of statistical queries; four generation conditions were judged against the expert gold hypotheses.
