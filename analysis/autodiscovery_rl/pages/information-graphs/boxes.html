Boxes Information Graph POC

Hypothesis generation as structured search over conditional-association queries H(A,B | N) on the BLADE boxes experiment — 629 children in seven societies choosing between majority- and minority-demonstrated options. The outcome is a derived binary (followed the majority); culture is categorical and conditioning-only. Gold hypotheses are the expert relationships (IV/DV/controls/interaction) extracted from the BLADE analyst annotations.

Every policy had the same budget of queries (a policy stops early if it exhausts its reachable space). Structure shows up in what the queries bought: distinct pairs covered, independencies found, and candidate edges pruned — the discoveries that shrink the remaining hypothesis space.
