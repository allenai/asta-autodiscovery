Executor sets the reward band — a KL leash is the lever that moves it

Original six arms: three executors (mini, gpt‑5.6, luna) × two losses, all at KL‑coef 0.001. Reward split by executor (mini ~0.24, gpt‑5.6 ~0.06); grpo vs cispo barely differ. New: a mini lr/KL sweep shows what actually moves reward within an executor — raising lr alone (2e‑6 / 3e‑6, KL 0.001) stays flat ~0.22, but 5e‑6 + KL 0.03 climbs to ~0.34 (peak 0.45). The KL leash lets a hotter lr move reward while holding it stable — note its KL‑loss drifts far higher (~0.15 vs ~0.004).

Read (original arms): reward shows two bands — mini ~0.24 and gpt‑5.6 ~0.06 — set by how often each executor clears the reviewer gate. Loss choice (solid vs dashed) moves nothing within a band. luna·cispo is 4 steps (stub).
Read (new mini lr/KL sweep): at fixed executor (mini), lr alone doesn't move reward — 2e‑6 and 3e‑6 (KL 0.001) sit flat at ~0.22, length bounded. Adding a KL 0.03 leash at 5e‑6 is the mover: reward rises ~0.22→0.34 (EMA), peaking 0.45. Its KL‑loss climbs to ~0.15 (vs ~0.004 for the KL‑0.001 arms) — the policy genuinely moves, but the leash keeps it stable rather than exploding. Grad‑norm stays ~0.9 (one spike to 1.6). Takeaway: the reward lever isn't the loss or lr alone — it's a hotter lr held by a KL leash.
