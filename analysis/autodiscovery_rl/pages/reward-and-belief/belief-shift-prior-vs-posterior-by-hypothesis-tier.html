Belief shift, prior → posterior, by hypothesis tier

Each panel overlays the prior and posterior distribution of P(hypothesis true) for one tier. Grounded = the model writes variables in schema form — backticks, snake_case (fish_caught), quoted category values ('Mostly agile'), or coded levels (y = 2). High-level NL = prose concepts (gender, income) with no schema tokens. Shared y-axis (% of hypotheses per bin); the gap between the humps is the belief update the reward pays for.

Both tiers start from nearly the same prior (~0.62). The grounded tier's posterior lands slightly lower (mean 0.622→0.487) than the NL tier's (0.625→0.504) — a marginally larger prior→posterior move, consistent with grounded hypotheses earning a bit more surprise reward.
